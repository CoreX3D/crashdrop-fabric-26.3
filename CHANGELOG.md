# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- Initial standalone Fabric release for Minecraft 26.3.
- Shared server rounds with a smooth multiplier chart and crash animation.
- Item stakes, server-authoritative cash-out, and a default maximum multiplier of ×64.
- Persistent fractional remainders for matching items and components.
- Preserved item names, enchantments, durability, charge, and other components in payouts.
- Pending payouts when inventory space is unavailable and refunds of unresolved stakes on a normal shutdown.
- Configurable round settings, stake and payout limits, and blacklist.
- Russian and English interfaces.
- No dependency on IC2 or Upgrader.
